One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated.
`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader).

> You will soon receive unexpected money. It is $0.00, from the exchange, marked 'liquidation complete.'

15 words, 102 characters, one line.
